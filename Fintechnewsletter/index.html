Modern Treasury applies to the OCC to pursue a bank charter

Payments infrastructure company Modern Treasury has applied to the U.S. Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) to establish itself as a regulated bank, adding its own banking layer on top of its existing infrastructure business. The move follows a pattern of fintech infrastructure players seeking direct bank charters rather than relying solely on partner-bank relationships.

Stripe data shows Europe's AI-commerce payment volume lagging the US

Stripe co-founder and CEO Patrick Collison shared a three-year chart of payment volume from AI businesses processed on Stripe, comparing the US and Europe, and the newsletter describes the two lines as diverging sharply, with Europe well behind. OECD: one in three European workers bound by a non-compete

The same newsletter cites new OECD research finding that one in three workers in Europe is bound by a non-compete clause, and that non-competes are linked to lower job mobility, slower wage growth and weaker productivity across Europe. It also notes Europe has about 325,000 AI workers (matching the US in headcount), but only 35% of them work at digital-native tech companies versus 54% in the US, with 48% instead "retrofitting" incumbent industries.

Pushback on the "agentic bank run" thesis, and McKinsey's take on what's at stake

Apollo economist Torsten Slok's thesis that consumer AI agents could trigger an "agentic bank run" (automatically sweeping household cash into accounts paying 3.3% to 5.0%, instead of the 0.1% US national average) drew a detailed rebuttal in This Week in Fintech, arguing human switching friction makes mass agent-driven account-hopping unlikely any time soon. Separately, the same week's issue cites McKinsey's Global Payments Report estimating that agentic AI could put $75 billion of global payments revenue at risk by 2030 (via idle-cash migration and customers avoiding card fees and interest), while also estimating gen/agentic AI could deliver up to $110 billion in annual productivity gains for payments providers.

Source: This Week in Fintech (Nik Milanović; Fundraises edition), October 2-3, 2026

Open USD stablecoin launches with Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, Shopify and Coinbase
